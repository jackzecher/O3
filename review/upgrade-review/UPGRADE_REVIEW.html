What changed

  • Mail opens directly to Unanswered, Waiting for replies and All mail. Incomplete evidence is visible; no hidden age or sender gate determines what appears.
  • No reply needed and Stop waiting are reversible local choices. New messages can bring work back. Full conversations and your place in a longer list remain reachable.
  • People, Documents, Calendar and Projects have direct destinations. Creating or changing a project updates its connections from information already saved.
  • Customization uses names, choices and real previews. Saved work and complex legacy definitions remain intact.
  • Appearance adds Harbor, Warm Paper, Sage, Slate and Night. The application icon is integrated; editable icon, wordmark and startup designs are included.

Screen-reader qualification and production-data validation remain separate from the native keyboard, UIA, contrast and synthetic-data checks performed here.
